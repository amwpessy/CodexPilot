# Lynncat Pilot Privacy Policy / 林猫驾驶舱隐私政策

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

## English

Lynncat Pilot is a local macOS utility that displays coding-quota signals, system status, disk read/write activity, and disk growth.

### Local Data Access

Lynncat Pilot may access the following information on your computer:

- System status, including CPU usage, memory usage, battery status, disk capacity, and disk read/write counters.
- Readable local coding-session log files, when available, to display quota-related information.
- Local monitoring history generated by the app to calculate since-launch disk and disk I/O changes.

These monitoring details remain on your device. Lynncat Pilot does not upload local session logs, quota events, system metrics, or disk I/O metrics.

### Optional Account and Community Data

Monitoring does not require an account. If you choose Sign in with Apple and use community, leaderboard, points, or game features, the service processes:

- The Sign in with Apple identity token needed to authenticate your account.
- An account identifier, authentication session token, nickname, and leaderboard visibility preference.
- Community posts and reports that you submit.
- Account activity used to award participation points.
- Point balances, card-draw records, collection progress, and completion-reward records used for the Water Margin card collection.
- A client identifier used for session security, reporting, and duplicate-request prevention.

This information is linked to your account and used only to provide app functionality, prevent abuse, maintain point balances, and operate the community and leaderboard. It is not used for advertising.

### Tracking and Analytics

Lynncat Pilot does not track you across apps or websites and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

### Local Storage

The app may store monitoring history, preferences, previously saved folder security bookmarks, blocked-author selections, and an authentication session token on your device. Authentication tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.

### Quota Information

Quota signals are read only from compatible local session events that are readable by the app. If the installed coding assistant does not write compatible quota information, the app reports that quota information is unavailable.

### Third Parties

Lynncat Pilot is an independent utility. It does not provide or resell access to any third-party coding-assistant service.

### Contact

For privacy questions, contact `amwpessy@gmail.com`.

## 简体中文

林猫驾驶舱是一款本地 macOS 工具，用于显示编码额度信号、系统状态、硬盘读写活动与硬盘增长。

### 本地数据访问

林猫驾驶舱可能会在你的电脑上访问以下信息：

- 系统状态，包括 CPU 使用率、内存使用率、电池状态、硬盘容量和硬盘读写计数。
- 应用可读取的本地编码会话日志；当兼容事件存在时，用于显示额度相关信息。
- 应用在本机生成的监控历史，用于计算从打开软件开始的硬盘和硬盘读写变化。

这些监控信息保留在你的设备上。林猫驾驶舱不会上传本地会话日志、额度事件、系统指标或硬盘读写指标。

### 可选账户与社区数据

监控功能无需账户。如果你选择使用 Apple 登录，并使用社区、排行榜、积分或游戏功能，服务会处理：

- 用于验证账户身份的 Apple 登录身份令牌。
- 账户标识符、认证会话令牌、昵称和排行榜显示偏好。
- 你提交的社区发言与举报。
- 用于发放参与积分的账户活动。
- 用于运行水浒卡册的积分余额、抽卡记录、收集进度与集齐奖励记录。
- 用于会话安全、举报和防止重复请求的客户端标识符。

这些信息与你的账户关联，仅用于提供应用功能、防止滥用、维护积分余额以及运行社区和排行榜，不用于广告。

### 跟踪与分析

林猫驾驶舱不会跨 App 或网站跟踪你，也不包含广告 SDK 或第三方分析 SDK。

### 本地存储

应用可能会在设备上保存监控历史、偏好设置、以前保存的文件夹安全书签、已屏蔽作者列表和认证会话令牌。认证令牌保存在 macOS 钥匙串中。

### 额度信息

额度信号仅从应用可读取的兼容本地会话事件中获取。如果已安装的编码助手没有写入兼容的额度信息，应用会明确显示额度信息不可用。

### 第三方关系

林猫驾驶舱是一款独立工具，不提供或转售任何第三方编码助手服务的访问权限。

### 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请联系 `amwpessy@gmail.com`。
